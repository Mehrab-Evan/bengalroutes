BENGAL ROUTES WEBSITE
=====================

Files:
- index.html: responsive, single-page business website
- bengal_routes_website_logo.png: logo artwork cropped from the supplied business card

Preview:
Open index.html in a modern browser. The website is static and does not require a server.

Contact form:
The inquiry form validates required fields and opens the visitor's default email application
with a prefilled message addressed to bengal.routes@gmail.com. It does not store submissions
or send email automatically.

Publishing:
Upload index.html and bengal_routes_website_logo.png together to your hosting provider's
public web directory (often named public_html or www). Configure www.bengalroutes.com
with your domain/hosting provider. The package is not published online.

Branding:
Navy blue and orange palette; supplied Bengal Routes business-card logo.
